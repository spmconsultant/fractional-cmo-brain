# Playbooks

Playbooks are run sheets: laws, principles and strategies in order, with an owner and a KPI for each step.

**The test for a playbook:** your team can run it without anyone in the room.

When a user wants to act, turn the relevant laws and strategies into a run sheet: numbered steps, an owner for each step, and one KPI to watch. Mark clearly which steps come from the playbooks below and which are your own application of the laws.

---

## Playbook: Clicks but no leads
**KPI:** real leads per month (whole numbers, counted from the CRM)

1. **Verify the number.** Pull leads from the CRM by date and source. Filter spam and junk referral traffic. (Law 15) Owner: marketing lead.
2. **Audit conversions.** List every conversion action and what it measures. Move engagement goals and any fractional counts out of the lead count. (Laws 14 and 16) Owner: ads manager.
3. **Run the Diagnostic Order:** impression share, then time on site by channel, then pages per visit, then the call to action and offer. Owner: ads manager.
4. **Fix one thing at the first failing check,** and record the baseline. (Law 3) Owner: whoever owns that asset.
5. **Measure for a fixed period, then lock the gain or revert.** Owner: marketing lead.
6. **Only then consider budget,** and only up to where incremental CPA meets allowable CPA. (Law 6) Owner: owner or CMO.

## Playbook: Headline and homepage rewrite
**KPI:** impression-to-click rate (ads) or engagement on the page

1. Run the First-Sentence Test on the current opening line. (Law 7)
2. Delete any claim a competitor could make word for word. (Law 4)
3. Rewrite the opening around the consequence the buyer fears. (Law 10)
4. Make sure the image tells the same story as the headline.
5. Check that every number is current and anchored to a fixed date. (Law 12)
6. Launch as a single-variable test against the old version. (Law 3)

## Playbook: Be the Answer (AI visibility)
**KPI:** whether AI assistants name the business for its core buyer questions

1. List the questions buyers actually ask before hiring.
2. For each, put a plain, quotable answer in the first 150 words of a page. (Law 19)
3. Make the facts match everywhere: website, Google Business Profile, social bios, directories. (One Story Everywhere)
4. Replace stale and undated claims. (Law 12)
5. Re-check the AI answers for those questions every quarter.

## Playbook: Own the Keys audit
**KPI:** number of core accounts with owner-level access held by the business (target: all 8)

1. List the domain registrar, website hosting, Google Business Profile, GA4, Search Console, Google Ads, Meta Business Manager and the CRM.
2. For each, record who holds owner or admin access.
3. Request owner access for any account the business doesn't control, framed as "someone is helping me review performance." (Law 17)
4. Do this before any audit, strategy or budget decision.
