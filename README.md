## How it works

The Brain is a Claude skill. When you ask a marketing question, Claude loads the skill and:

1. Asks about your business, one question at a time, if it doesn't know it yet.
2. Finds the **JRcmo Law** that governs your situation, named like *JRcmo Law #16 — Don't Trust the Fraction*.
3. Tells you what's breaking it and gives you the fix and a next step.

Everything runs inside Claude. There's no account to create, no connector, and no data leaves your conversation.

## Troubleshooting

- **Claude isn't using the Brain.** Check that the skill is turned on under **Customize → Skills**, then start a new conversation. You can also ask directly: "Use the Fractional CMO Brain to answer this."
- **Answers feel generic.** Give Claude specifics: your industry, revenue range, monthly marketing budget, and channels. The framework works best with real numbers.
- **Skill doesn't appear after install.** Refresh Claude or restart the desktop app. In Claude Code, run `/plugin` to confirm it's installed and enabled.
